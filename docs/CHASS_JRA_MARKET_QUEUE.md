# Phase107: independent MARKET queue and reason audit

This patch delivers an initially disabled MARKET scheduler. It does not enable
the scheduler, provision production storage, or authorize Production Activation.
DATA selection, DATA maxJobs=1, the DATA-only EARLY reader, and signal generation
are unchanged. The existing MARKET bridge remains configured as before.

The new runner reads the fresh official meeting independently and discovers all
races. It rotates the whole list, inspects previously frozen MARKET and DATA
availability, excludes frozen/ineligible races from the attempt allowance, and
continues to the next eligible race after HOLD. It never takes DATA inspectedJobs
or DATA planning limits. The existing bridge still validates identity, complete
odds, timestamps, snapshot hashes, and the atomic first-market insert.

## Separate configuration and rollout

The new path is OFF unless ENABLE_JRA_MARKET_QUEUE is exactly `true`.
Before a separately authorized production trial:

1. Review CI and merge this implementation.
2. Provision migration 0013_jra_market_queue_audit.sql separately; this patch
   does not run a migration. A missing audit table blocks the new runner before
   any MARKET save.
3. Measure inspection, ledger writes, bridge attempts and total run duration.
   Choose explicit positive integer strings for JRA_MARKET_QUEUE_MAX_INSPECTIONS,
   JRA_MARKET_QUEUE_MAX_ATTEMPTS and JRA_MARKET_QUEUE_WINDOW_MS. No production
   defaults or fixed 24-race allowance are supplied without measurements.
4. Disable ENABLE_JRA_PRECOMPUTED_MARKET_BRIDGE before enabling the new path.
   The new runner rejects simultaneous legacy activation. Keep official-cache
   source mode. Do not change DATA limits or the formal EARLY contract.

Worker scheduling runs the separate queue after the existing scheduled task
promise succeeds. Its clock and budget start when its own runner starts; DATA
does not supply its candidate list or cap. A rejection of the outer task promise
can still prevent invocation; no ledger for that invocation means unknown.

The deadline gates inspection and MARKET acquisition/save admission. In-flight
D1 calls cannot be cancelled, and terminal audit writes can finish after the
deadline. The window is not a hard upper bound on the entire Cron duration.
Discovery, inspection and audit write overhead consume the acquisition window.
Existing bridge checks before saving remain in force.

## Evidence and limitations

The append-only ledger records runId, eventNo, targetDate, raceId, selectionTurn,
queueRank, recordedAt, attemptedAt, selection, save outcome and elapsed time.
DISCOVERED precedes inspection. ATTEMPT_STARTED is persisted before calling the
bridge. A ledger failure stops further attempts. TERMINAL records include
FROZEN, ALREADY_FROZEN, DATA_MISSING, ODDS_UNAVAILABLE, ODDS_INCOMPLETE,
ODDS_IDENTITY_OR_TIME, POST_TIME_UNKNOWN, POST_TIME_REACHED, SAVE_CONFLICT,
NOT_SELECTED_LIMIT, NOT_SELECTED_INSPECTION_LIMIT and NOT_SELECTED_DEADLINE.
Other bridge validation reasons are preserved rather than collapsed.

An attempt selected just before its start record exhausts the budget may have
DEADLINE without a bridge call. attemptedCount counts actual calls, while
selected indicates admission to an attempt. Neither indicates a successful save.
An interrupted attempt or runner exception has an unknown save outcome (`null`),
not an invented odds failure. The actual MARKET snapshot audit remains the
authoritative evidence of persisted Freeze.

The read-only production audit adds the latest recorded queue run and validates
event hashes, indexed fields, sequence and lifecycle. Missing/invalid ledger
storage produces UNAVAILABLE without changing the existing snapshot coverage
report. The ledger cannot reconstruct pre-Phase107 reasons or invocations that
never recorded anything; an interrupted discovery may omit undiscovered races.
Retention and production D1/runtime costs require separate operational review.

Tests demonstrate synthetic 24-race throughput and real local SQLite bridge
behavior, including HOLD fall-through, already-frozen preservation, repeated
runs, limits and incomplete audit evidence. They do not establish production
24/24 throughput or production limit values. No marks, expected value, diamonds,
warnings, formal KPI activation or Live updates are generated by this patch.
